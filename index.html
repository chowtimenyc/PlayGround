THE LOOP

LIGHT ×4 builds the hit chain. HEAVY cancels into a stagger. LAUNCHER pops enemies up, then jump and keep hitting. DODGE cancels almost anything.

Style Rank climbs from D to SSS. Higher ranks mean more damage, more shards, and more swagger. Taking a hit cuts your style. Taunting buys you a little back.

UNLOCK

Every run earns Soul Shards. Spend them in the Roster to unlock six additional fighters, or in Skills to permanently empower each character.
